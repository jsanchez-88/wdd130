Rivière Sauvage

Our purpose is to provide unforgettable rafting experiences while connecting people with nature and adventure. To offer safe, exciting, and memorable river adventures for individuals, families, and friends. We believe in teamwork, respect for nature, safety, and making every adventure count. Vivez l'aventure, domptez les rapides ! "Live the Adventure, Conquer the Rapids!"

History

Rivière Sauvage was founded by a group of adventure lovers who wanted to share their passion for rivers and outdoor activities. What started as a small local adventure business has grown into a place where families and friends can enjoy exciting rafting experiences while creating unforgettable memories.

We believe that rafting is more than just navigating a river. It is an opportunity to build friendships, develop teamwork, challenge ourselves, and appreciate the beauty of the outdoors. At Rivière Sauvage, every journey is a new opportunity to discover something amazing and create memories that last a lifetime.
