El Salvador

El Salvador, the smallest and most densely populated country in Central America, is a land of dramatic landscapes and even more remarkable people. Tucked between Guatemala and Honduras, it stretches south to meet the Pacific Ocean, where volcanic peaks give way to black-sand beaches and lush, rolling lowlands.
